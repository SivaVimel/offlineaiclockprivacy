Privacy Policy

Offline AI Clock for iPhone and iPad · last updated 22 September 2026

Offline AI Clock collects nothing, sends nothing and stores nothing outside your device. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no network code. Everything below is detail on that.

This is checkable, not just claimed

A privacy policy is only worth what can be verified against it. Two things here can be, without taking anybody's word:

  • Turn on Airplane Mode before you open the app. Every feature works identically, including the assistant. Nothing degrades, because nothing was ever going anywhere.
  • The app is not built with networking in it. Not "networking that is switched off" — the shipped binary does not link iOS's networking frameworks at all. There is no code in it that could open a connection.

What the app stores, and where

Your alarms, timers, stopwatch, pinned cities and settings are written to the app's own private storage on your device. They are not copied anywhere else. Deleting the app deletes them.

WhatWhere it is kept
AlarmsA file in the app's private container, plus a second copy beside it so a damaged file cannot lose your alarms silently
TimersA file in the app's private container
Pinned cities, settings, stopwatchThe app's own preferences
What you type to the assistantIn memory only. It is never written to disk, and it is gone when you clear the conversation or the app closes
Scheduled alarms and timersHeld by iOS on the device — the time and the label, nothing else
Reminder notificationsThe iOS notification system, on the device

If an alarm file is ever found damaged — after a device restore, say — the app keeps the damaged copy rather than deleting it, so nothing you set can disappear without trace, and it tells you on the Alarms screen that it did so. That file stays in the app's private container like everything else, and goes when the app does.

The assistant

The language model is a file inside the app and runs on your device. What you type is turned into a prompt on the device, answered on the device, and never transmitted. There is no server, no API key and nothing to sign in to.

Two things it does not do, which are worth saying plainly because similar apps do them: it does not use what you type to train anything, and it does not keep a history. Each question is answered on its own and then forgotten.

The app itself — not the model — works out your alarms, timers, durations and time-zone answers. Any sentence the model writes that contains a figure is discarded before you see it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or email address.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or telemetry of any kind. No analytics, advertising or attribution SDK is embedded — the only third-party code in the app is the model runtime listed below, which has no network access of any kind.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers or tracking. Nothing is shared with anyone, so App Tracking Transparency does not apply and the app never shows that prompt.
  • No contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or health data. The app never asks for them, and there is no code in it that reads them.
  • No cloud service of its own. If you have iCloud device backup switched on, the app's files are included in that backup, which is Apple's service and subject to Apple's terms — not ours, because there is no ours.

Permissions

The app asks for exactly one thing: permission to send notifications, the first time you switch an alarm on, because an alarm cannot ring without it. The notifications are created and scheduled on your device. They are not push notifications; no server is involved and nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Setting a real alarm also asks for alarm permission, which you grant from a banner on the Alarms screen. Alarms and timers are then held by iOS itself so they can ring with the app closed. What iOS is given is the time it should ring and the label you typed — nothing else, and nowhere but this device.

Both permissions can be withdrawn at any time in iOS Settings. Without them the app keeps working; only the ringing gets weaker.

Children and age rating

The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, and has no way to. It does include an assistant powered by a language model that answers what you type, which is reflected in its App Store age rating — please see the rating on the App Store listing for the current classification.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights of access, correction, deletion and portability over personal data a company holds about you. No personal data about you is held, received or processed by us at any point, so there is nothing to disclose, correct, export or erase on request.

The data the app creates lives on your device and is yours already: deleting the app removes all of it, immediately and completely.
